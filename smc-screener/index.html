Strategy settings (saved in your browser)

WAITING = limit order level not touched yet. TRIGGERED = entry touched within the last 3 closed candles (check price before chasing). “Coin backtest” is the same rules run over that coin’s last ~1000 candles (in-sample, after fees) — a sanity check, not a promise.

5 checks: DMI (+DI>−DI and +DI>ADX), RSI>EMA(RSI,14), RSI>50, OBV>EMA50, Open Interest rising 2 bars in a row. Score ≥4 = aligned. Our gold backtest of this score showed no reliable edge on its own — use it as a filter, not as a signal.

Pullback = full EMA stack, last 3 candles touched EMA21 (within 0.25 ATR) and the latest close is back on the trend side. Our 23-coin 1H backtest of this pullback found no consistent edge (0 of 119 configs positive in all 3 folds) — it is a screener view, not a validated signal.

Share = sector’s portion of total 24h quote volume across scanned coins, compared with the average share of the previous 7 closed days. Participation = % of coins in the sector that are up in 24h. Sector tags are a static, hand-made list; unlisted coins fall under “Other”.

Run it yourself — live backtest in your browser

Runs the current strategy settings over the last ~1000 candles of every coin loaded by the scan (fees included). This is in-sample on recent data.

Honest caveats

  • Win rate ≈ 40% at RR 2.5 is normal for this kind of strategy: profit comes from winners being 2.5× the losers, so losing streaks of 6–8 trades will happen.
  • Coins are highly correlated, so 19 coins ≠ 19 independent proofs.
  • Fills are assumed at the limit price; real slippage, funding and missed fills will reduce results.
  • No signal here is financial advice or a guaranteed accuracy figure. Paper-trade first and size small.

Strategy logic

  1. Structure: a swing high/low is confirmed after n candles on each side.
  2. Break: a close beyond the last swing sets the bias. Same direction as before = BOS, opposite = CHoCH.
  3. FVG: within N bars of the break, a 3-candle gap in the break direction is the entry zone.
  4. Order: limit entry at the chosen depth inside the gap. SL = far edge of the gap ± buffer×ATR. TP = RR × risk. One order is dropped if price closes through the zone or it expires.
