BEFORE YOU ASK

Straight answers

Is this financial advice?

No. It is a screener and educational tool. Crypto derivatives carry a substantial risk of loss and the backtests found no proven edge. Trade your own plan.

Do I need to log in or pay?

No. There is no account, no payment and no sign-up. Open the dashboard and it works.

Where does the data come from?

Binance's public futures API, fetched directly by your browser. Only closed candles are used, so signals do not repaint.

Does it place trades for me?

No. It never touches your exchange account. It only shows levels.

How are signal results tracked?

Each signal is saved in your browser and replayed on later closed candles: entry triggered, TP1, TP2, TP3 or SL. If the stop is hit after TP1 it shows “TP1 HIT → then SL HIT” (or “stopped at entry (BE)” if you enabled the break-even option). It only updates while the page is open.

Why does a coin show as ✘ on some checks?

Because signals with 6 of 8 checks are shown by default. You can require all 8 from the filter, or loosen it to 4.
