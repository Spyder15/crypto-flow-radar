How an FVG setup is built
  1. Structure: a swing high/low is confirmed after n candles on each side.
  2. Break: a close beyond the last swing sets the bias (same direction as before = BOS, opposite = CHoCH).
  3. FVG: within N bars of the break, a 3-candle gap in the break direction is the entry zone.
  4. Order: limit entry inside the gap, SL beyond the far edge ± buffer×ATR, TP = RR × risk. Cancelled if a candle closes through the zone, or after the validity window.
Other caveats
  • Coins are highly correlated; many coins ≠ many independent tests.
  • Fills assume the limit price is reached; real slippage and funding make results worse.
  • Nothing here is financial advice.
